# PROJECT BRIEF: DESIGN AND DEVELOP THE OFFICIAL WEBSITE FOR HIGHFLY AI

## 1. Your Role

Act as a senior UI/UX designer, brand strategist, frontend engineer, full-stack developer, and SEO specialist.

Design and develop a complete, modern, premium, responsive, production-ready website for **HighFly AI**, a software development and artificial intelligence solutions company.

The website must communicate trust, technical expertise, innovation, creativity, and business value. It should appeal to startups, small and medium-sized businesses, educational institutions, hospitality businesses, retailers, and organizations looking to automate or digitize their operations.

Do not create only a static design or a generic template. Build a cohesive, functional website with reusable components, realistic content, working navigation, responsive layouts, project detail pages or sections, and a functional contact workflow.

If you have access to the uploaded HighFly AI logo, use it as the primary brand asset. Analyze its colors and visual identity before choosing the final palette. Do not replace or redesign the logo unless explicitly requested.

## 3. Brand Design and Visual Direction

Create a distinctive visual identity for HighFly AI.

### Color palette

First inspect the provided logo and use its actual colors wherever possible.

If the logo colors cannot be reliably extracted, use the following provisional palette:

- Deep Navy: #10172A
- Electric Blue: #4169F5
- Soft Sky Blue: #8BA9FF
- Cloud White: #F5F7FC
- Slate: #242F46
- Pure White: #FFFFFF

Use deep navy for the hero section, selected navigation elements, footer, and premium dark sections. Use blue for interactive elements, highlights, icons, and important calls to action. Use white and soft gray backgrounds to maintain readability.

The provisional palette must not override the uploaded logo's actual colors.

### Design style

Create a polished technology-company website inspired by high-quality SaaS and digital product studios.

Use:
- Strong typography and clear visual hierarchy
- Generous whitespace
- Clean grids and consistent spacing
- Subtle blue gradients
- Elegant cards with restrained shadows
- Rounded corners used consistently
- Modern line icons
- Product dashboard mockups or genuine project screenshots
- Subtle hover states and restrained animation
- Smooth scrolling where appropriate
- Accessible color contrast
- Responsive layouts for mobile, tablet, laptop, and large desktop screens

Use a modern font such as Inter, Manrope, or a comparable sans-serif typeface.

Avoid excessive glassmorphism, distracting animations, random stock imagery, overly rounded cards everywhere, cluttered layouts, and generic AI-generated illustrations that do not support the content.

The site should feel like a real technology company with its own identity, not a premade agency template.
